Download PNG · Download SVG · PlantUML source

What the logical level captures

  • Describes 3NF-normalised relations with full attributes, primary keys (PK) and foreign keys (FK).
  • Data types stay abstract (Integer, String, DateTime, Boolean, Text, Decimal) – independent of any DBMS.
  • Junction tables for many-to-many links: exam_questions, session_exams, session_slot_exams, exam_slot_template_exams, enrollments, session_enrollments.
  • Business rules are explicit: UNIQUE, CHECK, delete behaviour and concurrency tokens.
