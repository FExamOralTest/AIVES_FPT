What the conceptual level captures

  • Describes only business entities and their relationships – no attributes, no keys, no data types.
  • Meant for business stakeholders (lecturers, department head) to agree on system scope.
  • Four business areas: Catalog & Content, Exam Organisation, Taking & Grading, Enrolment & Audit.
  • 20 business entities. The three many-to-many junction tables (session_exams, exam_slot_template_exams, session_slot_exams) are folded into a single conceptual entity, Exam Assignment, to keep the diagram readable.
