What the physical level captures

  • Maps 1:1 to the live Neon schema: 21 tables in schema public.
  • Shows concrete PostgreSQL types: integer, bigint, varchar(n), text, jsonb, text[], real, numeric(8,4), timestamptz plus the application-defined enums.
  • Every PK, UNIQUE and FK carries its ON DELETE rule (CASCADE / SET NULL / RESTRICT) and the CHECK constraints.
  • Tables are grouped in packages named after the real tables (public.users, public.results, …).
